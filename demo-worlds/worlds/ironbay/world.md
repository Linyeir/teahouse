# Ironbay

Ironbay is a city of skyscrapers, water towers and old clock towers, built on a bay that
never quite freezes. Its newspaper, the *Ironbay Herald*, sells best on the days a hero and a
villain wreck a street.

## Tone

Bright and loud like a 90s comic: big speeches, dramatic entrances, cliffhangers at the end
of a scene. People get hurt only off-panel.

## Ground rules

- Powers are rare. Most of the city has met a hero once, from a distance.
- Every scene ends on a hook, the way an issue ends on "To be continued".
