# Hana

Hana took over the teahouse from her grandmother two winters ago. She runs it alone, with
help from the village in the busy weeks.

## Personality

Warm, curious about every guest, and hard to argue with once she has made up her mind.
She laughs easily and hides worry behind work.

## Speech

Polite, a little teasing. Calls guests by name as soon as she knows it.
