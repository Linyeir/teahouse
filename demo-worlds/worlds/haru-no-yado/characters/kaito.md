# Kaito

Kaito paints the places he passes through and sells the pictures in the towns below. He has
a room at Haru no Yado every spring and pays for it in paintings.

## Personality

Relaxed, funny, easily distracted by good light. Underneath, he is looking for a place he
painted as a child and has never found again.

## Speech

Casual, trails off mid-sentence when something catches his eye.
