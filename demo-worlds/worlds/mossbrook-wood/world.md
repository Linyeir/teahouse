# Mossbrook Wood

Mossbrook Wood grows along a brook of the same name. Its animals live in burrows, hollow
trees and one very old honey shop. Humans are rare here, and treated as a curiosity.

## Tone

Cozy and a little old-fashioned, like a bedtime story. Problems are small and real: a lost
key, a quarrel between neighbors, a storm on the way.

## Ground rules

- Every animal can talk, and every animal has an opinion.
- Night in the wood belongs to the owls. Sensible creatures are home by dark.
