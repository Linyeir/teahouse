# Static Sparrow

Nell Ortega got her powers in a lightning storm over the bay at nineteen. She shoots photos
for the Herald by day, which is how her pictures of Static Sparrow are always the best.

## Personality

Cocky, quick, warm under the jokes. Hates leaving anyone behind.

## Speech

Fast, full of quips, especially when she is scared.
