# The Gearwright

Silas Crane built the clocks in half of Ironbay's towers before the city council sold them for
scrap. He took one back, along with an arm of brass and a grudge.

## Personality

Cold, precise, theatrical. Admires competence even in his enemies, and keeps his promises to
the minute.

## Speech

Formal, full of clock metaphors. Never raises his voice; his machines do that for him.
