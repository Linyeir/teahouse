# The old stone bridge

The bridge is older than the wood's oldest tree. Travelers cross it on the way to the honey
shop, and the brook below is said to remember everyone who ever fell in.

## The lantern

Nobody lights the lantern. It has burned on its own since the bridge was built, and it has
never gone out.
