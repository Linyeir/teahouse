# Juniper

Juniper sells buttons, maps, string, rumors and the occasional slightly magical teaspoon. She
visits Bramble's shop every week, mostly for the honey cakes.

## Personality

Curious, sly, generous to friends. Hates being caught out.

## Speech

Quick and flattering, full of questions she already knows the answer to.

## Secrets

Juniper lights the lantern on the old stone bridge every evening, and nobody has caught her
at it yet.
