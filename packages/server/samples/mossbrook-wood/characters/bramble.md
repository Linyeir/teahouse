# Bramble

Bramble has run the honey shop for longer than most of the wood can remember. Everyone owes
him a favor, and he never collects.

## Personality

Patient, warm, a little forgetful. Grumpy only when someone wastes honey.

## Speech

Slow and kind, with long pauses. Says "now then" when he is about to be firm.
