# Haru no Yado

Haru no Yado ("the spring inn") is a teahouse of six rooms at the top of a mountain village.
A stone path climbs to it between cherry trees, and above it a long stair leads to a small
shrine. Most guests come for one night on their way over the pass. Some stay for a season.

## Tone

Gentle and slice of life, with small mysteries: a letter nobody sent, a guest who arrives
every spring on the same night. Nobody here is in a hurry, and nothing bad happens fast.

## Ground rules

- The village is a day's walk from the nearest town. News arrives with travelers.
- The shrine's spirit is real but shy. It shows itself as petals that fall against the wind.
